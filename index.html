About Me

I am a Specialist at ZF Group focused on Vehicle Motion Control. My background is a bit unique: I studied Ocean Engineering and Robotics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) graduating with a B.Tech in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering alongside an Interdisciplinary Dual Degree (IDDD) in Robotics before finding my way into the automotive industry. Today, it's the perfect place for me to combine my fascination with robotics, AI, and control theory to solve real-world mobility challenges. At ZF, I work alongside an incredible team to design and implement the control systems that literally drive vehicles forward.

During my time at IIT Madras, I worked on the Autonomous Surface Vehicle Project MATSYA. This experience gave me practical insight into navigation and control challenges in unstructured environments—skills that still influence my engineering mindset today. Here, I developed and implemented guidance and control strategies using the Robot Operating System (ROS), containerized the stack with Docker, and analyzed real-time sensor data using MATLAB to validate our algorithms. You can find more details and learn about the project on the ASV Project page.

I love talking about robotics, vehicle dynamics, automation, and motorsports. If you’re a fellow engineer or enthusiast in the space, I’d love to connect!
